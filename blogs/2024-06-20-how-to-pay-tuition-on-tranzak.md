How to pay school fees using Tranzak FOLLOW THE FOLOWING STEPS IN OTHER TO PAY TUITION FEES USING TRANZAK: -Search for school -Select “ Pay tuition ” to continue -Fill student details and select “Pay now” -Choose from already existing payment methods or add one -Authorise payment -A payment will be shown after succesful transaction -The payment information will be displayed on the homepage HAPPY TRANZAKING!!!
